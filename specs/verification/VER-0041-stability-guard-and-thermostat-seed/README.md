## DR-008 (`check_stability`)
Design: `src/measure/check_stability.{cu,cuh}`, an `Action` evaluated in `end_of_step` every `interval` steps; T, U, max|F|, max|q| by one fixed-grid reduction kernel with a deterministic host combine (NaN/inf count as violations); shortest distance by a dedicated cell-list kernel independent of the potential (explicit image shifts, triclinic, open directions); charges via the existing `Potential::get_charge_reference()` (no new accessor); a device ring buffer of the last `keep_frames` evaluated frames; on violation: message, `stability_frames.xyz`, `PRINT_INPUT_ERROR` exit 1. U0 is the potential energy at the first evaluation of each `run`. T uses 3N degrees of freedom (no fixed-atom or COM correction).

## Limitations / not verified
- HIP build; AC-5 target (< 1 %) not met at 256 atoms (+1.4 %), met within the noise at 39 304 atoms; per-criterion cost not resolved there; multi-GPU refusal only with a patched scratch build; `npt_scr` seed after-warm-up check is within 1e-9, not bitwise; the guard is not tested on PTFE (AC-8, polymer-apps).
- U0 is taken at the first evaluation (step = interval): a collapse before it, or a slow drift across `run` commands, is invisible; T is not corrected for fixed atoms; minimum distance is O(N^2) when the threshold is much larger than the box (correct, slow).
- gpumd's LJ neighbour list hangs for cells thinner than about 1 A (observed), so thinner cells cannot be tested through the executable.

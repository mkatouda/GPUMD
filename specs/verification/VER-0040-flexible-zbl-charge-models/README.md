## Findings recorded, not changed
1. **Angular-list truncation**: ZBL in NEP/qNEP kernels uses the angular neighbour list; a pair with outer cutoff > `rc_angular` is truncated (energy discontinuity). Same in ordinary NEP. Now documented in `zbl.rst` and warned for flexible qNEP in gpumd (and replica, verbose).
2. **Non-charge_transfer qNEP charge definition**: `nep` writes `charge_train.out` as the raw ANN output; gpumd dumps the neutralised charge (`zero_total_charge`); they differ by a per-structure constant. Identical in the pre-change binaries and upstream master; charge_transfer models are neutral by construction and agree (VER-0035 Gate 3a-3: 1e-7..1.6e-6). Existing behaviour, record only; the train test compares charges only for charge_transfer.
3. Trainer with more than 10 elements overruns the ZBL buffer: pre-existing, not touched.
